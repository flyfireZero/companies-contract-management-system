# Companies Contract Management System (CCMS)

An enterprise-grade, AI-powered Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) system designed to automate contract ingestion, clause analysis, multi-stage approval workflows, expiration forecasting, and risk intelligence.

## Vision & Capabilities
- **AI Document Analysis**: Automated parsing of complex legal agreements (PDF, DOCX, TXT) with intelligent extraction of key clauses (Indemnity, Liability, Termination, Payment, Confidentiality).
- **Comprehensive Lifecycle Management**: Full state machine governance from initial drafting, peer review, legal approval, e-signature tracking, active monitoring, to automated renewal or termination.
- **Automated Expiry & Milestone Radar**: 30/60/90-day alert windows, auto-renewal trigger prevention, and proactive notification dispatchers.
- **Enterprise Risk Scoring**: Heuristic & NLP-based risk evaluation flagging uncapped liability, unilateral terminations, and compliance gaps.
- **Portfolio Analytics & Statistics**: Real-time aggregation of Total Contract Value (TCV), Annual Recurring Revenue/Expense (ARR), counterparty exposure, and stage cycle times.
