MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of December 1, 2025 ("Effective Date"), by and between Apex Systems Inc ("Customer") and Horizon Cloud Technologies LLC ("Vendor").

Section 1. Confidentiality
Each party agrees to hold the other party's Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose such Confidential Information to third parties for a period of five (5) years following termination.

Section 2. Limitation of Liability
Except for breaches of Section 1 or gross negligence, neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. The total aggregate liability of either party arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total amounts paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

Section 3. Indemnification
Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer against any third-party claims alleging that the software or deliverables infringe any valid copyright, patent, or trademark.

Section 4. Payment Terms and Invoicing
Vendor shall invoice Customer on a monthly basis. Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within Net 30 days of receipt.

Section 5. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days prior written notice. Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party breaches any material term and fails to cure within thirty (30) days.

Section 6. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
